Huemeld — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

The short version: Huemeld has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of ours that collects your data. Your puzzle progress lives on your device (with a private backup in your own iCloud / device backup). The app never shows forced ads: you can choose to watch a video ad from Google AdMob for a hint or to unlock more levels. Google may use a device identifier for those ads if you allow tracking. Buying "Huemeld Pro" unlocks everything without videos.

What we store on your device

Huemeld saves your game locally (level progress, daily-puzzle streak, settings, and purchase unlock flags) using your device's local storage. We never receive this data.

So that progress survives deleting and reinstalling the app, the iOS/Android app also keeps a copy of your progress and settings (not purchases) in the app's private storage, in iCloud key-value storage on your own Apple account (iOS), and in Android's automatic device backup to your own Google account (Android). Only the app can read it; we never see it. Turning off iCloud for Huemeld (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud) or Android backup stops this.

Advertising (optional videos)

The iOS/Android app has no forced ads. Ads appear only when you tap to watch one: a short rewarded video to reveal a hint, or to unlock the next batch of pack levels. These videos are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, Google may collect device information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, if you grant permission), IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data. On iOS we ask for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, videos still play but are not personalized using your identifier.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is loaded. You can change that choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy choices.

Google's practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosures.

Purchases

The in-app purchase ("Huemeld Pro"; the retired "Remove Ads" is still honoured for past buyers) is processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them across reinstalls; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase receipt, not your name or payment info. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, emails, or passwords.
  • No analytics SDKs beyond what AdMob/RevenueCat need to function.
  • No selling of personal data.
  • No collection of contacts, photos, location, or microphone/camera data.

Children

Huemeld is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured through Google AdMob's standard general-audience settings.

The web version

The version playable in a browser at izgebayyurt.github.io shows no ads and contains no ad or purchase SDKs; it only uses local storage for progress. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads (Google Fonts privacy FAQ). It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub's privacy statement applies to hosting logs).

Your choices

  • Avoid ads: simply don't tap "watch a video"; or buy Huemeld Pro; or play the web version.
  • Change ad consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): in the app, Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Withdraw tracking consent (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Erase your data: delete the app (and, to remove the backup copy, turn off iCloud for Huemeld / delete it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage; or clear site data in your browser).
