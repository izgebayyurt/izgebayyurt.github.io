Huemeld — Support

Mix colors. Fill the board.

Need a hand? Email izgebayyurt@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. Please include your device model and iOS version, and — if it's about a purchase — the email tied to your App Store account.

How to play

Draw a line out of each glowing circle. Where two lines meet in a junction, their colors meld into a new one — red + yellow makes orange, blue + yellow makes green, and so on. Light up every square with the right color and cover the whole board to win. Stuck? Tap Hint to reveal one pipe, or Undo / Reset to try again.

Purchases

I bought Huemeld Pro but levels are still locked.

Open Settings (the gear icon) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID (or Google account on Android) you bought with. If it still doesn't apply, email us with your purchase date and we'll sort it out.

How do I restore purchases on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID (or Google account), install Huemeld, then Settings → Restore Purchases. Huemeld Pro (and the older Remove Ads) restore for free.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone/iPad) or via Google Play's refund page (Android).

My progress

I deleted the app — is my progress gone?

If you're signed into iCloud, Huemeld backs up your progress to your private iCloud key-value store. Reinstall on the same Apple ID and your levels, streak, and settings come back on first launch. Progress is also kept locally, so it survives app updates. Purchases aren't part of that backup — tap Settings → Restore Purchases to bring Huemeld Pro back.

Hints & ads

How do hints work?

Tap Hint to reveal a ghost of one pipe. On the free version you watch a short video to earn a hint; with Huemeld Pro (or the older Remove Ads), hints are free with no video.

Does Huemeld show ads?

Never uninvited. There are no ads between levels. A video plays only when you choose one — to reveal a hint, or to unlock the next 8 levels of a pack. Huemeld Pro unlocks everything, so you never need a video.

"No video available right now"?

No ad could load (offline, or none available at that moment). Wait a few seconds and tap again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In Europe (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Settings → Privacy choices reopens Google's consent form. On iPhone, tracking permission is under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Settings

Settings (gear icon) has sound and music toggles, separate sound and music volume sliders, a light/dark theme switch, and color-blind labels that mark each color with a letter. The language (English / Türkçe) is on the main menu.

Found a bug, or a level you think can't be solved?

Every level is verified solvable, but if one has you convinced otherwise, email us the level number (shown at the top of the board) and a screenshot — izgebayyurt@gmail.com.
