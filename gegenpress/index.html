gegenpress

turn-based soccer tactics

Every opponent move is shown before you commit. No dice, no randomness — read the telegraph, put bodies in the right squares, win the ball back and punish them. Fourteen turns. Draws don't count.

The ladder

A tactics puzzle in the spirit of Into the Breach. Everything is deterministic — if you lose the ball, it's because of a square you chose.

how to play

The turn

  • At the start of every turn the opposition declares everything it will do — runs, passes, shots, tackles — drawn on the pitch in red. They are locked into it.
  • Then you move. Each of your players may move once and act once, in any order you like. Tap a player, tap a square, then pick an action.
  • Press End Turn and their declared plan resolves. Anything you blocked, intercepted or dodged simply fails.
  • Reset rewinds the whole turn. It's a puzzle — experiment freely.
  • On a keyboard: Tab cycles players, Space ends the turn, U undoes a move, R resets, Esc cancels an action.

Winning the ball

  • Tackle needs two of your players adjacent to the carrier. One body is not enough — a lone tackle fails and leaves that player beaten (no turn next round).
  • Your Anchor tackles alone, but only goal-side — it has to be in front of the carrier, between them and your goal. Chasing from behind does not win the ball. Their Enforcers work the same way.
  • Your Sweeper can Shove an adjacent opponent one square. Shove a carrier over the touchline and the throw-in is yours.
  • Passes and shots travel in straight rays. Any body standing in the lane intercepts or blocks it — that's most of your defending.

Scoring

  • Shots are rays too — straight or diagonal, toward the goal. Every shot shows its verdict up front: GOAL, SAVED or BLOCKED.
  • A keeper's reach shrinks as you get closer. From distance it covers the whole goal; from point-blank it only saves the exact square it stands on. Get in the box.
  • A keeper that rushes off its line saves nothing but the squares it physically occupies.
  • Their keeper picks its spot by reading whoever has the ball right now. Pass to a teammate who hasn't acted yet, then shoot with them — the keeper committed to the wrong player. That combo is the whole game.

Your squad

  • Anchor — tackles alone from goal-side. Your ball-winner.
  • Sweeper — fastest legs, and the Shove.
  • Playmaker — longest shot range, and a Threaded pass that slips past the first body in the lane.
  • Keeper — stays near its box, Claims any ball within one square.
