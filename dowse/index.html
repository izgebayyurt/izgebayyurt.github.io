How Dowse works
Tap a cell to lay down whatever the round button is set to, and tap again to clear it. Double tap for the other one. Drag to fill a run in one go. Tap a number to see how far its reading reaches. Pinch to zoom and two fingers to pan; Fit puts the whole board back on the screen.
